Base Plate

Real Gorilla Tag movement. You have no legs: your hands are what touch the world. A hand that touches the ground sticks there, so moving it pushes your body the other way, and you keep that speed when you let go.

Left click / Right click (or Q / E) hold to reach out with your left / right hand, toward where you're looking.

Look at the ground ahead, reach, then flick the mouse down to shove yourself forward. Do it with alternating hands to run, or slam both down quickly to jump.

Walk sim (on by default): WASD knuckle-walk, Shift sprint, Space jump, C crouch. Press Tab to switch between walk sim and hands.
